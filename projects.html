Chess Coach

Work in progress

A chess coaching app that runs on your own computer. It connects to your Chess.com or Lichess account, studies the games you have actually played, and turns them into personal lessons: a guided replay of where each game turned, practice puzzles built from your own missed chances, and a coach you can ask about any position. Every piece of advice is checked against a chess engine before you see it, so it points to a real moment in one of your games instead of offering generic tips. It also estimates your playing strength from your moves and tracks how it changes over time.
